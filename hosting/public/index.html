Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Scrabble Companion is an offline-first Android app for turn timing, word checking, and score keeping. This page explains what data we handle — and what we do not.

Offline first

Scrabble Companion is designed to be fully functional without an internet connection. We do not transmit your game data, scores, or settings to any external servers.

Local data storage

All game history and personal preferences are stored locally on your device. This data remains on your phone and is not accessible by us.

Analytics & tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising identifiers. Your use of the app is private.

Permissions

The app requires only the minimal permissions needed to function. We do not access your contacts, location, or camera.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback by email or WhatsApp, we will only use your contact details to respond to your enquiry. We do not share your contact information with third parties.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because gameplay data stays on-device, there is no remote profile or account for a child to create.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app’s behaviour changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest revision.
